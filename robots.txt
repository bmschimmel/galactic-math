# Galactic Math crawler policy
#
# Search engines and AI search / assistant bots are welcome -- a free kids'
# math game is worth finding. AI *training* crawlers are not. This file is
# the version-controlled statement of that stance; Cloudflare's "Configure AI
# bot policies" setting (Search: Allow, Training: Disallow AI Training,
# Agent: Allow) enforces it at the edge. See docs/overview.md.
#
# Content Signals (https://contentsignals.org/) are machine-readable usage
# preferences read alongside the User-agent rules:
#   search   = building a search index and returning links / short excerpts
#   ai-input = feeding pages into an AI model at answer time (RAG, grounding)
#   ai-train = training or fine-tuning AI models
# Any restriction expressed here is an express reservation of rights under
# Article 4 of EU Directive 2019/790 on Copyright in the Digital Single Market.

User-agent: *
Content-Signal: search=yes, ai-input=yes, ai-train=no
Allow: /
